Codec settings

Our neural QP increases bitrate as it rises. Conventional codec QP works in the opposite direction. Higher resolutions may run below real-time speed.

MLVC encoder and decoder devices can be selected separately. At 1080p, running both neural codecs on GPU may exceed available memory.

Fast cross-device mode uses ONNX networks with bitstream checks for entropy indexes and reference features. The decoder repairs small device differences automatically. It is experimental; other devices or content may exceed the repair limit. CPU inference remains slower than GPU inference.
